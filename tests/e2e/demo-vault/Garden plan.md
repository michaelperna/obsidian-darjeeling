Planning the spring and summer vegetable garden in raised beds.

- [ ] Carrots (Danvers Half Long)

### Companion Planting Notes
- Plant basil next to tomatoes to deter pests.
- Marigolds around bed perimeters for nematode control.
- Drip irrigation on a 30-minute morning timer.
